Smart Thermostat 16.85 - 10/12/2026

- Redesigned the Family Center display in Kid Mode for households with multiple children.
- Removed the single DEVICE LOCKED replacement card from the former Alarmo position; the Alarmo area now remains intentionally open in Kid Mode.
- Kid Mode now shows a dedicated Kids Status Board below the main thermostat controls.
- Every child receives an independent card with name, points, and available privilege icons.
- If a child's device is locked, only that child's card turns red and shows that child's Family Center lock reason.
- Unlocked children show DEVICE AVAILABLE in their own green/cyan status area.
- Supports up to six child cards: 1-3 display in one row, 4 display as 2x2, and 5-6 display as 3x2.
- Normal non-Kid-Mode Family Center and Alarmo layout is unchanged.
