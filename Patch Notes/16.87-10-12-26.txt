Smart Thermostat 16.87 - 10/12/2026

- Reworked the Kid Mode Family Center status board into a compact vertical stack on the far right side of the thermostat screen.
- Reduced Kid Mode family-card width, padding, font size, privilege icon size, and lock-status footprint so the cards no longer overlap the thermostat dial.
- Each kid still keeps an independent card with name, points, privileges, and that kid's lock reason.
- Added Home Assistant capability detection for dimmable versus power-only lights.
- Power-only lights hide the brightness slider and show simple ON/OFF status instead of an artificial 0% brightness state.
- Power-only light commands no longer send brightness_pct or color data.
- The Lights page can now use switch.* Home Assistant entities as power-only lights in addition to light.* entities.
- Added conservative self-repair for unavailable/stale light mappings: when one matching live replacement exists, including a switch-domain replacement, the saved card is updated automatically.
- Light state rendering now follows Home Assistant's actual on/off state instead of treating brightness=0 as proof that the light is off.
