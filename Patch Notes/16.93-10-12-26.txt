Smart Thermostat 16.93 - 2026-10-12

Changed
- Added 0.5 F run-through hysteresis to normal thermostat operation.
- Cooling that is already running now continues until 0.5 F below the target.
  Example: target 74 F -> stop cooling at 73.5 F.
- Heating that is already running now continues until 0.5 F above the target.
  Example: target 70 F -> stop heating at 70.5 F.
- The configured Temperature Differential still controls when a new heat/cool
  call starts; this patch does not repurpose or overwrite that setting.
- Safety-temperature operation continues to use exact safety thresholds.
- Existing minimum runtime, minimum off-time, fan hold, external HA ownership,
  and 16.90-16.92 persistence/reconciliation protections remain unchanged.

Reason
- With an exact target shutdown, cooling could stop at exactly the setpoint
  (for example 74.0 F), then the sensor could rebound to 74.1 F while compressor
  minimum-off protection prevented an immediate restart. The 0.5 F run-through
  gives the system a stable deadband before shutting equipment down.
