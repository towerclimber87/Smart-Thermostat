Smart Thermostat 16.89 - 10/12/2026

- Fixed power-only Home Assistant light toggles such as light.guest_bathroom.
- ON/OFF-only lights now fetch their fresh Home Assistant state before every tap and send the opposite clean power command.
- Power-only lights no longer use cached brightness/card state to decide whether to turn on or off.
- Dimmable/RGB light behavior is unchanged.
